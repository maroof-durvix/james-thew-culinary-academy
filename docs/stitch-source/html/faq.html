Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about membership tiers, recipes, tip exchanges, contest rules, and academy access.

search

Click ‘Join Now’ on any page or navigation bar, select your preferred pass (Monthly at $10/month or Yearly at $100/year), complete the secure payment process, and fill out your account registration details. Once confirmed, your account gains instantaneous access to our entire vault of 50+ Michelin-grade premium recipes, chef notes, and member features.

Yes. We offer two transparent subscription passes: Monthly ($10) or Yearly ($100, saving $20) for full catalog access and exclusive executive formulations. Foundational basic recipes and free tips remain complimentary forever with no charges or registration required.

Free recipes and tips can be explored freely without charge or registration by visiting the Free Recipes and Free Tips sections. The full premium catalog—including executive masterclasses, exact sous-vide temperature curves, and secret ingredient ratios—requires an active paid membership.

Yes, general users and guests can participate freely by submitting their original recipes or tips to any active contest. Our culinary challenges are open to everyone to encourage domestic gastronomic talent.

Logged-in members can simply use the ‘+ Add Recipe’ or ‘+ Add Tip’ actions accessible directly from their member dashboard or the recipe library. Fill in your ingredient quantities, step-by-step procedures, and upload a dish photo for community publication.

You can post recipe feedback and tasting notes directly on each individual recipe page under the ‘Community Feedback’ section. For overall website suggestions, academy queries, or general observations, please use our dedicated Give Feedback page.

Contest winners are officially announced on the Announcements page and archived in Past Winners following comprehensive review and adjudication by Chef James Thew and the executive tasting panel. Winners are also notified via their registered contact email.
