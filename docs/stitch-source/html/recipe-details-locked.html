Culinary Procedure

Precision Guide
01 Foundation Step

Tempering and Vacuum Infusion

Pat the Wagyu fillets completely dry using culinary linen. Season generously on all sides with cracked sea salt flakes. Place into a chamber vacuum pouch with fresh thyme sprigs...

02

Water Bath Equilibrium & Thermal Control

Submerge the sealed pouches into the calibrated immersion circulator set precisely to 53.5°C (128.3°F). Cook for 90 minutes to render the intramuscular fat without compromising the delicate cell architecture.

03

Reduction of Truffle Glace & Emulsion

In a copper saucier, sweat the shallots gently. Deglaze with aged Madeira and reduce until syrupy. Whisk in the enriched veal stock and simmer down to coating consistency before finishing with shaved black truffles.

04

Cast-Iron Maillard Sear & Plating

Remove fillets and flash-sear in a smoking hot cast-iron pan with clarified butter for strictly 45 seconds per facet. Rest for 4 minutes before carving against the grain.
