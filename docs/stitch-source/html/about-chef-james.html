01 // The Narrative
format_quote
“Cooking at the five-star level is not merely about executing precise heat and salt; it is the discipline of respecting each ingredient’s lineage and offering guests an authentic moment of transcendence.”
Chef James Thew

Heritage, Discipline & The Five-Star Standard

Trained across iconic classical brigades and modern European kitchens, Chef James brought his rigorous international discipline to Pakistan’s culinary capital, Karachi. Appointed Executive Chef at landmark five-star properties, including Mövenpick Hotel Karachi, he oversaw master brigade banquets, diplomatic receptions, and signature dining rooms that set benchmark standards nationwide.

His craft relies on ancestral techniques—slow coal embers, botanical macerations, and handcrafted stocks—recast through ultra-modern culinary chemistry. Every plate represents an intentional dialogue between Sindh’s spice riches, Mediterranean freshness, and fine French pastry precision.

Screen, Ink & Community Academy

Beyond luxury dining rooms, Chef James became a household mentor through his high-ratings national television series, demystifying complex five-star culinary chemistry for millions of home cooks. His televised masterclasses prioritize real pantry mastery, fundamental knife skills, and wholesome organic living.

His published volumes have earned international praise, chronicling centuries of aromatic subcontinental marinades, restorative raw juice formulations, and contemporary Italian pasta philosophies. Through his weekend cooking workshops, he continues to train future professional sous chefs and enthusiastic culinary devotees.

02 // The Trajectory
Milestones & Appointments
2018 — Present Executive Leadership

Executive Chef & Culinary Director • Mövenpick Hotel Karachi

Leading culinary operations spanning 5 luxury outlets, premier banqueting halls, and private head-of-state diplomatic dinners. Spearheaded the modern heritage farm-to-table initiative and implemented Michelin-standard kitchen ergonomics.

2020 — 2024 National Television

Host & Lead Chef • “The Master’s Hearth” (National Prime-Time TV)

Produced and hosted over 180 episodes of prime-time weekend cooking pedagogy. Welcomed guest celebrity restaurateurs, conducted regional ingredient exposés, and guided viewers through technical sous-vide and patisserie fundamentals.

2014 — 2018 Hospitality Craft

Chef de Cuisine • Pearl Continental Luxury Suites

Commanded signature Italian and Continental dining rooms. Curated bespoke botanical juice pairings and introduced organic cold-press menu architectures that garnered the National Hospitality Gold Laurel.

2021 — 2025 Gastronomic Literature

Cookbook Author & Culinary Research Scholar

Authored three internationally reviewed monographs detailing heirloom sauce science, whole-plant gastronomy, and modern artisanal pastry structures.
