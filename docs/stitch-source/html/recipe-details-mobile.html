Truffle Infused Wild Mushroom Risotto

schedule Prep 25m
kettle Cook 40m
star 4.9 (64)

The Masterclass Summary

A symphony of earthy porcini, velvety Carnaroli rice, and aromatic black truffle essence finished with aged Parmigiano-Reggiano. Crafted for the discerning palate seeking true culinary intimacy.

Precision Ingredients

Serves 2

Culinary Procedure

Stage 01 12 Mins

The Mushroom Sauté & Infusion

Slice wild mushrooms uniformly. Sauté in noisette butter over medium-high flame until deeply caramelized. Reserve half for plating garnish.

lightbulb

Chef's Note: Do not overcrowd the pan; high heat ensures moisture evaporates completely, concentrating the umami core.

Stage 02 18 Mins

Tasting the Toast & Gradual Addition

Toast the Carnaroli rice dry until translucent edges appear. Deglaze with dry white wine, then ladle simmering broth one measure at a time while continuously agitating.

lightbulb

Chef's Note: Gentle, rhythmic stirring releases the starch naturally, giving you that quintessential velvety emulsion without cream.

Stage 03 10 Mins

Mantecatura & Truffle Finish

Remove from heat. Vigorously fold in cold butter, grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, and a precise drizzle of truffle oil. Rest for two minutes covered.

lightbulb

Chef's Note: Never let the risotto boil after adding the cheese; thermal shock ruins the glossy emulsion.

A Note from Chef James

Executive Culinary Director

"Risotto is a meditation in patience. Treat the grains with respect, listen to the quiet simmer of your stockpot, and do not rush the mantecatura. When plated correctly, it should flow gracefully across the dish like a wave."
